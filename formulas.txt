formulas : 
	1]to calculate areas of : 
			i)square :          (side*side)
			ii)rectangle :    (length*breadth)		
			iii)circle :          (3.14*(radius*radius))
				         (circumference of circle : 2*(3.14*radius))
			iv)triangle :      (0.5*(base*hgt))
			v)cuboid :         (2*(len*wid)+2*(len*hgt)+2*(hgt*wid))

	2]to volume of : 
			i)box/cuboid :	 (len*wid*hgt)
			ii)sphere         :	 (4/3*(radius*radius*radius))
			iii)cylinder     :	(3.14*radius*radius*hgt)
			iv)cone            :	((3.14*radius*radius)*(hgt/3))
			
	3]to perimeter : 
			i)rectangle : (2*(len*bre))
			ii)square    : (4*side)
			iii)triangle  : (side1+side2+side3)
			iv)cylinder : (2*(2*radius + hgt)) 	//or (4*r + 2* hgt)
